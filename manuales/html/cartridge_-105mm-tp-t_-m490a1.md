## Description

This cartridge is for use in 105mm tank cannon M68 for training in marksmanship.

The cartridge is the same in external appearance as the [basic M490](cartridge_-105mm-tp-t_-m490.html). However, internally it differs from the M490 in that the projectile has no fin assembly and is static stabilized. The projectile body is one inch longer. Some M490A1’s may be assembled with the spiral wrapped cartridge case. The standoff spike is steel, not aluminum, and the obturator has no seal.

The propellant in the cartridge case is the M14 and not the M30 as in the M490 cartridge. The cartridge case is fitted with the electric primer M80A1 instead of the M83.

The projectile is painted blue with white markings.

## Functioning

The electrically initiated primer ignites the propelling charge. Gases produced by the burning propellant propel the projectile from the gun and ignite the tracer which burns for a minimum of 2.5 seconds

## Hazardous Components

Cartridge case - M148A1B1, M148A2B1

Propelling charge - M14

Primer - M80A1

Tracer - M13

## Physical Data

Length, overall - 39.34 in, 999.2 mm
