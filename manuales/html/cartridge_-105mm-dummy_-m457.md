## Description

This dummy cartridge is used as a drill round to train tank crews in handling ammunition and loading the 105mm gun cannon.

The cartridge simulates a loaded round of 105mm high explosive plastic ammunition in size, weight, and center of gravity. The projectile is of steel, and is secured to the cartridge case by crimping. A steel weight is assembled to the rear of the projectile and is held in place with a spring pin.

The projectile is blue with white markings. 

## Functioning

The cartridge is completely inert and does not function.
