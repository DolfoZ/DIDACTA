## Description

These two practice rounds differ from the [high explosive rounds](cartridge_-105mm-he_-m38_-m38a.html) in regard to filler only. The filler consists of 3.09 pounds of an inert filler made of lead oxide, paraffin, and barium carbonate accompanied by a [black powder](black-powder.html) charge. This black powder charge is 8 ounces in the M38A1 Round, while it is only 5 ounces in the M38 Round. The components are the same as those in the high explosive rounds.
