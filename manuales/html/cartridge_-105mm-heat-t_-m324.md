## Description

HEAT-T cartridge M324 is employed against armored targets. 

This cartridge has a projectile consisting of a boattailed steel body fitted with a pre-engraved gilding metal rotating band, two indexing buttons and a steel windshield. The projectile is loaded with 3.06 pounds of [Composition B](composition-b.html) around a conical copper liner. The liner and windshield are held in place by a threaded steel adapter. The projectile base is fitted with a BD fuze which contains an integral tracer. The steel cartridge case, which is crimped to the projectile, contains a propelling charge protected by a rayon and plastic liner and a press fitted percussion primer.

## Hazardous Components

3.06 lb Composition B main charge. M32 cartridge case, M57 primer, M10 propelling charge (8.21 lb)
