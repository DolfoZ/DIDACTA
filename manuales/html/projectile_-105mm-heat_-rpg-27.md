## Description

This is a fin stabilized, tube launched, high explosive antitank (HEAT) projectile used against armored targets. The figure shows appearance and dimensions of the projectile. 

The warhead is painted olive green with black markings. The motor section is covered by an unpainted wrapped fiberglass protective coating. Other material, filler and weight are unknown at this time.
