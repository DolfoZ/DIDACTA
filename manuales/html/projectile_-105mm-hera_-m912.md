## Description

This is a spin stabilized, high explosive rocket assisted (HERA) projectile for an extended range capability. 

The projectile is painted olive drab with cartridge designation markings stenciled in yellow. The caliber, designation, and lot number of the projectile are stamped into the rotating band.
