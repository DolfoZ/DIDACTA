## Description

HE Cartridge M323 is used for blast, fragmentation and mining. 

This cartridge is composed of a steel projectile assembled with a pre engraved gilding metal rotating band and two indexing buttons. It contains a bursting charge of 4.38 pounds of [TNT](tnt.html). This is a deep cavity projectile, containing a supplementary charge of .3 pound of TNT. The projectile may be assembled with either an impact or mechanical time impact fuze. The projectile is crimped to a perforated steel cartridge case. The propelling charge is packed in a rayon and' plastic liner. A percussion primer is press fitted into the base of the cartridge case.

## Hazardous Components

Main charge is 4.38 lbs of TNT. M32 cartridge case, M57 primer, M10 propelling charge (7.95 lb)
