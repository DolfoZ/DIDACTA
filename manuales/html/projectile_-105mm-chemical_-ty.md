## Description

The length of the round was 48.76 centimeters (19.2 inches) with the fuze and 43.94 centimeters (17.3 inches) without the fuze. It had one rotating band, which was 1.50 centimeter (0.59 inch) wide. The cartridge case may have been brass-coated steel, unpainted steel, or black steel spirally bound and marked with the characters 6342/65C.
