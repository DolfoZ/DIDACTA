## Description

This round was used in the M1917, M1917A1 and M1918 howitzers. As with the [M110 round](projectile_-155mm-chemical_-m3.html), the chemical fill was loaded into the round and the burster well tube was pressed into place. This configuration minimized the likelihood of leakage. This round was developed from the [Mk IIA1 limited standard chemical round](projectile_-155mm-chemical_-mk.html).

The M105B1 round had a hemi-spherical base in the inner cavity while the M105 round had a flat base. No cartridge case was used with this round. The breech of the howitzer acts as the case.
