## Description

This cartridge is for use in training in marksmanship. The cartridge is similar to and ballistically matched to [HEAT-T cartridge M456E1](cartridge_-105mm-heat-t_-m456a.html); however the projectile is inert loaded, except for the tracer, and assembled with an inert fuze.
