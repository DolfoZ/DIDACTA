## Description

This cartridge is for use in 105mm gun cannons for training in marksmanship.

The cartridge is similar in external appearance and ballistically similar to [HEAT-T cartridge M456 series](cartridge_-105mm-heat-t_-m456a.html). The projectile consists of a steel body, an aluminum standoff spike, and a boom and fin assembly with tracer. The cartridge case is filled with loosely packed propellant and is fitted with an electric primer.

The projectile is painted blue with white markings.

## Functioning

The electrically initiated primer ignites the propelling charge. Gases produced by the burning propellant propel the projectile from the gun and ignite the tracer which burns for a minimum of 2.5 seconds.

## Hazardous Components

Cartridge case - M148A1B1, M148A1

Propelling charge - M30

Primer - M83

Tracer - M13

## Physical Data

Length, overall - 39 in, 990.6 mm
