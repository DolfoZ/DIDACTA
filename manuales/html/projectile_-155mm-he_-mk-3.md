## Description

This was the first high explosive round to be developed for 155mm guns. The complete round, as fired, consists of the projectile fuzed either with [PD M46](pd_-m46.html) or [M47 Fuze](pd_-m47.html), the propelling charge for 155-mm Guns M1917-17A1-18M1, and either a [Mk II or Mk IIA 21-grain Percussion Primer](primer_-percussion_-mk-2a1.html).

The Mk Ill Round was modified by adapting the nose of the projectile to take new fuzes and boosters. Complete round of Mk. IIIA1, as fired, consists of the following:

The projectile fuzed with either the Selective Fuze M51 with [Booster M21](booster_-m21a4.html), or Fuze M51A1 with Booster M21A1; or [Fuze, Time, Mechanical, M67](mt_-m67a3.html), with Boosser M21A1.
