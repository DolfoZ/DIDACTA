## Description

The test and dummy projectiles are externally similar to the [M712 tactical projectile](projectile_-155mm-heat-guided_.html). This is a high-explosive antitank (HEAT) cannon-launched guided projectile (CLGP). The projectile is self-contained, having a laser seeker homing system and integral fuzing. The projectile is setback-armed and impact-fired with a graze-sensitive piezoelectric crystal (Lucky) firing system. 

The guidance, warhead, and control sections may be painted in various colors and patterns, depending upon the test assignment. Projectiles with a high-explosive or spotting charge warhead will have yellow markings and/or a yellow band.
